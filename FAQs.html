FAQ

Partner FAQ

How white-label works with LinkLiftPro, plus answers about links, SEO, websites, ads and payments.

White-label partnership

How does white-label delivery work?

You sell the service under your brand. We do the work and send unbranded reports for you to forward.

Will you ever contact my clients?

No, unless you explicitly ask us to join a call.

Can you use my report template?

Yes. We can fill your template or supply unbranded reports.

Links & SEO

How are publishers vetted?

We check organic traffic trends, relevance, spam signals and outbound linking before adding a site.

How fast are guest posts delivered?

Typically 5–10 working days after content approval.

Is there a replacement guarantee?

Yes. Removed links are replaced free within 6 months.

Do you handle full SEO retainers?

Yes: audits, on-page, technical, content, local SEO and links.

Websites

Which platforms do you build on?

WordPress, Shopify, Webflow and custom code.

Can you work from my designer's files?

Yes, we build from Figma or Adobe XD designs.

Who owns the site?

Your client, once the project is paid.

Paid ads

Do you manage ads in client accounts?

Yes. Campaigns run in your clients' accounts with your agency as the face of the work.

Which platforms?

Google, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and TikTok.

Is ad spend included?

No. Clients pay platforms directly; our fee covers management.

Pricing & payment

Do you offer wholesale pricing?

Yes, partner pricing is designed to leave room for your margin.

How do I pay?

PayPal, Payoneer, Wise or card. Larger projects can be split into milestones.
